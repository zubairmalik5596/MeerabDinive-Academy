Course Overview

The Tajweed Mastery course takes students who can already read the Quran and refines their recitation according to the classical rules of Tajweed. Over guided one-to-one sessions, students learn correct articulation points (Makharij), rules of elongation, and the etiquette of recitation — building toward a recitation that is both technically correct and spiritually engaged.

What You Will Learn

  • Correct articulation points (Makharij al-Huruf) for every letter
  • Rules of Noon Sakinah, Meem Sakinah, and Madd
  • Qalqalah, Ghunnah, and rules of stopping (Waqf)
  • Applied recitation practice with tutor correction each session

Course Features

Live 1-to-1 Classes

Weekly private sessions with a certified Tajweed tutor.

Progress Reports

Monthly summaries sent directly to parents or students.

Recorded Sessions

Review past classes anytime for extra practice.

Who Is This Course For

Students age 8 and above who can already read Quranic text and want to refine their recitation — whether preparing for Hifz, improving for Salah, or simply reciting with more confidence.

Learning Benefits

Beyond technical accuracy, Tajweed builds a deeper mindfulness during recitation, connecting students more closely to the meaning and rhythm of each verse — a skill that stays with them for life.
