Who we are

Knowledge That Guides Beyond the Classroom

Meerab Divine was founded on a simple belief: that learning the Quran should feel personal, unhurried, and rooted in genuine understanding. Our tutors don't just teach recitation — they build a relationship with every student, tracking progress with the same care a family would.

Today, students across more than 20 countries join our live one-to-one classes each week, guided by scholars trained in Tajweed, Tafsir, and classical Arabic.
