Our story

A Classroom Without Borders

Meerab Divine began in 2014 with a single tutor and a handful of students learning Nazra over video calls. What started as a way to make quality Quran education accessible to families abroad has grown into a full academy of over 60 tutors, teaching more than 2,500 students across five continents.

We've kept the same principle from day one: every student deserves a tutor who knows them, tracks their progress, and adjusts the pace to fit their life — not the other way around.

Our mission

Making Authentic Islamic Education Accessible to Every Home

We exist to remove the barriers — distance, scheduling, uneven quality — that keep families from quality Quran instruction, replacing them with structured, personal, and trustworthy learning.

Our vision

A Generation Rooted in Knowledge and Character

We look toward a future where Islamic learning sits comfortably alongside modern life — where students carry the Quran not as a subject they studied, but as a companion for life.

Why it matters

Why Islamic Education Matters Today

In a fast-changing world, grounding a child in Quranic knowledge gives them something steady to return to — a source of guidance, identity, and moral clarity that lasts well beyond the classroom.

  • Builds a lifelong personal relationship with the Quran
  • Strengthens identity, discipline, and moral grounding
  • Creates community and belonging among students worldwide
